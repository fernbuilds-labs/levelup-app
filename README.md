# Level Up

**Level Up** is a free habit tracker that installs on your phone like an app. It opens on today: a progress ring, the block of your day you're in right now, and your habits grouped by time of day. Tick habits or count them (glasses of water, minutes read), skip with a reason without breaking a streak, and see your week as a grid. Analytics show trends, a year heatmap, which habits lift the rest of your day, and progress toward big goals, with PDF and spreadsheet reports for any week, month or year.

A two-minute setup gets you started: pick a starter template (working professional, shift worker, student, musician or creative, wellness basics, or blank), set your wake and sleep times, and choose which modules you want: daily schedule, sleep log, XP and a reward shop, and big goals.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

**Open it:** https://fernbuilds-labs.github.io/levelup-app/

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, keep **Open as Web App** on, and tap **Add**.
3. Open it once from the home screen while online so it saves itself for offline use.

## Install on Android

1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**) and confirm.
3. Open it once while online so it saves itself for offline use.

## Your data

- Check-ins, settings and rewards are stored on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Sync tab:** export a backup file now and then, or import one to move to a new phone. Days merge and the most recent edit wins.
- Updates never erase data. Deleting the app from your home screen does, so export first.

## Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Use **Send feedback** on the Sync tab, or open an issue in this repository.

## For the maintainer

When any file changes, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` (the build script does this from `src/VERSION`). The app checks for a new version each time it opens and reloads itself when one is found.
